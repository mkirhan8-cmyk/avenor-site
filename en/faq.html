Ordering

Ordering and price

How do I place a request?

Fill in the “Request a quote” form below, or write to info@avenor.com.ua or on WhatsApp: product, volume, port and basis. We send an indicative price usually within one business day.

What does the indicative price depend on?

Product, volume, packing, destination port, basis and shipment month. Final price, quantity, quality and basis are set only by a signed contract and specification.

Which delivery terms do you offer?

FCA, CPT, FOB, CFR, DAP, as agreed in each contract. Who does what — see How to order.

Delivery

Routes and transit time

Which route to China is the fastest?

Via Romanian ports: approx. 60–75 days from warehouse to a port in China (per carrier schedules). For other routes the time is given in the offer.

Can you ship from the port of Odesa?

We run coasters from the Danube ports and Odesa; a container from Odesa — on request. The main container route now is via Romanian ports and Gdańsk.

Do you ship to the EU and Türkiye?

Türkiye: by truck and by vessel. EU: by sea to Greece and EU ports; by truck — on request.

Can you ship in big bags?

Yes, by truck to Türkiye; to the EU on request.

Quality

Quality and documents

What protein level does your meal have?

Typically from 35%; the minimum is stated in the contract specification. Other levels on request, depending on the plant.

Do you have pellets?

Yes, on request.

Who checks quality and weight?

An independent surveyor at loading, as agreed in the contract. The quality and weight certificate is in the document set.

What documents do you provide?

Quality, weight, origin and phytosanitary certificates, B/L or CMR. Full list — see Quality.

Risk

War and risk

What about war risk?

If the line adds a war-risk surcharge, who pays it is agreed in the contract; we confirm price and timing in the offer. If the main route is closed, we offer another.

Who is responsible for the cargo in transit?

We manage the cargo and documents; risk passes under the Incoterms rule in the contract.
