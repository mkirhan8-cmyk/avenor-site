Buyer FAQ

Answers to the main questions about ordering, terms, delivery, documents and risk. If yours is not here, write to us (below).

Ordering

How do I place a request?

Fill in the "Request a quote" form, or write to us at info@avenor.com.ua or on WhatsApp: product, volume, port and basis. We send an indicative price usually within one business day.

Price and delivery terms

What does the indicative price depend on?

It depends on the product, volume, packing, destination port, basis and shipment month — the same fields as in the request form. Final price, quantity, quality, delivery basis and other terms are set only by a signed contract and specification.

Which delivery terms do you offer?

FCA, CPT, FOB, CFR, DAP — as agreed in each contract.

Delivery

Which route to China is the fastest?

Via Romanian ports: approx. 60–75 days from warehouse to a port in China (per carrier schedules). For other routes the time is given in the offer; it depends on packing and shipment date.

Can you ship from the port of Odesa?

We run coasters from the Danube ports and Odesa; a container from Odesa — on request. The main container route now is via Romanian ports and Gdańsk.

Do you ship to the EU and Türkiye?

Türkiye: we ship by truck and by vessel. EU: by sea to Greece and EU ports — we run it; by truck — on request.

Can you ship in big bags?

Yes, by truck to Türkiye; the EU — on request.

Quality and documents

What protein level do you offer?

Typically from 35%; the minimum is stated in the contract specification. Other levels on request, depending on the plant.

Do you have pellets?

Yes, on request.

Who checks the quality?

An independent surveyor at loading; the quality and weight certificate is in the document set.

How do you check the weight?

At loading we check weight, seals and documents. An independent surveyor checks quality and weight at loading, as agreed in the contract.

What documents do you provide?

A full set: quality, weight, origin and phytosanitary certificates, B/L or CMR.

War and risk

What about war risk?

If the line adds a war surcharge, who pays it is agreed in the contract; we confirm price and timing in the offer. If the main route is closed, we offer another; price and timing are agreed separately.

Who is responsible for the cargo in transit?

We manage the cargo and documents; risk passes under the Incoterms rule in the contract.
