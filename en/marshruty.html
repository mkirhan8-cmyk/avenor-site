Risks

Risks and how we cover them

War: strikes on Black Sea and Danube ports and vesselsThere is always a second route: rail via Gdansk and trucks via Constanta do not depend on Ukrainian ports. We include insurance and the war-risk surcharge in the price, not after it.
Port closure or restrictionWe move to a neighbouring port (Constanta, Gdansk); time buffer is set in the contract.
Queues at the border and rail crossingsWe watch the queue daily and book a slot; for rail, the application is filed in advance.
Waiting time and demurrageLoading terms and free days are fixed in the contract; B/L scan within 1 business day.
Document delay, customs refusalWe check the set before dispatch; backup broker; decision on the day of refusal.
Quality and weightSurvey at loading, seals, act at release from the warehouse.

Questions

Buyer FAQ

Which route to China is faster?

Rail + sea and trucks + sea take similar time: ≈ 40–55 days. The choice depends on packing and booking date.

Can you ship from Odesa port?

We arrange a direct container on request; the main route now is via Constanta and Gdansk.

What about war risk?

We include it in the price in advance; there is always a second route.

Who is responsible for the cargo in transit?

We run the cargo and documents; responsibility follows the Incoterms term in the contract.

Do you ship to the EU and Türkiye?

Türkiye: by truck and vessel, on request. EU: we arrange trucks.

We choose the route and term for each deal. Availability of ports, borders and rail depends on wartime conditions and government decisions; we confirm the current option in the offer.
