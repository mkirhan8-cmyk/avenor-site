Terms

How we work

Basis

FCA · CPT · DAP · FOB · CFR (Incoterms® 2020), agreed for each contract.

Packing

Bulk (vessel, rail wagon), containers, big bags on trucks. Oil in bulk or in flexitanks.

Lot size

By agreement: from one truck or container to a vessel lot.

Payment

By agreement, set in the contract.

Surveyor

An independent surveyor checks quality and weight at loading, as agreed in the contract.

Documents

CMR or bill of lading, invoice, packing list, certificates of origin, quality and weight, and phytosanitary — by product and country.

About us

AVENOR TRADE LLC, Odesa

AVENOR TRADE is a Ukrainian exporter of agricultural commodities. We supply oilseed meals and cakes, vegetable oils, oilseeds, grains, pulses and sugar to Türkiye, the Middle East, the EU and Asia.

We buy from producers and processors in Ukraine and the region. Our team in Odesa manages each shipment from loading to the agreed delivery point.
