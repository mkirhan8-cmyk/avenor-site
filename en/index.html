Basis

FCA · CPT · DAP · FOB · CFR (Incoterms® 2020), agreed for each contract.

About us

AVENOR TRADE LLC, Odesa

AVENOR TRADE is a Ukrainian exporter of agricultural commodities. We supply oilseed meals and cakes, vegetable oils, oilseeds, grains, pulses and sugar to Türkiye, the Middle East, the EU and Asia.

We buy from producers and processors in Ukraine and the region. Our team in Odesa manages each shipment from loading to the agreed delivery point.
