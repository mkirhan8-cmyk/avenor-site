How to order

Send us the product, volume, port and basis. Indicative price usually within one business day.

Request a quoteWhatsApp

Process

4 steps

  1. Request

    Product, volume, destination port, basis, preferred timing.

  2. Indicative offer

    Route and indicative price. Final terms are set only in the signed contract.

  3. Contract and specification

    Price, quantity, quality and delivery basis are fixed in the contract and specification.

  4. Shipment and documents

    Weight, seals and documents are checked at loading. One point of contact until delivery.

Basis: who does what

BasisWeYouRisk passes to you
FCAHand the goods to your carrier at the agreed place, export clearanceCarriage, insurance (optional), import clearanceWhen the goods are handed to your carrier
CPTExport clearance, carriage to the agreed placeInsurance (optional), import clearance, unloadingWhen handed to the first carrier, still in the country of shipment
FOBGoods on board your vessel at the port of loading, export clearanceVessel, freight, insurance (optional), import clearanceWhen the goods are on board at the port of loading
CFRGoods on board, freight to your port, export clearanceInsurance (optional), unloading, import clearanceWhen on board at the port of loading, not at the port of destination
DAPExport clearance, delivery to the agreed placeUnloading, import clearanceAt the place of destination, before unloading

Under these terms neither party must insure the cargo; the contract states who insures and how. Reference only. Incoterms® is a registered trademark of the ICC.
